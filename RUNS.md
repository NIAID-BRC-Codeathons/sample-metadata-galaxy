# Runs

These three directories are Loom / Galaxy analysis runs. Each is driven by the source paper PDF at the top of the directory (experimental design, data accessions, and what to reproduce in Galaxy).

## project1v2 (main)

PDF: **Perturbation-specific transcriptional mapping for unbiased target elucidation of antibiotics** (`pnas.202409747.pdf`)

Primary Loom / Galaxy run for the Romano, Bagnall et al. *PNAS* 2024 PerSpecTM RNA-seq study (*P. aeruginosa* PA14; GEO GSE251671 / SRA PRJNA1055047).

## project1

PDF: **Perturbation-specific transcriptional mapping for unbiased target elucidation of antibiotics** (`pnas.202409747.pdf`)

Earlier pass at the same PerSpecTM paper as `project1v2`. Treat `project1v2` as the canonical run; this directory is the first attempt.

## proj_blasi_reanalysis

PDF: **Gene Expression Profiling of Pseudomonas aeruginosa Upon Exposure to Colistin and Tobramycin** (`fmicb-12-626715.pdf`)

Loom / Galaxy reanalysis of Cianciulli Sesso, Lilić, Bläsi et al. *Front. Microbiol.* 2021 (PA14 colistin / tobramycin RNA-seq; ENA PRJEB41029).
